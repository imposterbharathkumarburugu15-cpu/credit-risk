6. Decision rules

Decision policy. Let t be the cost-optimal threshold for the matrix above.
  • probability ≥ t → HIGH: decline or manual review
  • t/2 ≤ probability < t → MEDIUM: approve with a lower limit and monitoring
  • probability < t/2 → LOW: approve
